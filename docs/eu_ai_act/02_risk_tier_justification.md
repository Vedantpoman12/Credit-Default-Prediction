# 02 Risk Tier Justification

> Educational artefact, not for real lending decisions. Not deployed.

Annex III, point 5(b) of the EU AI Act lists AI systems used to evaluate the creditworthiness of natural persons or establish their credit score as **high-risk**. This system scores individual credit default risk, so it falls in that tier and would face the Chapter III Section 2 obligations (risk management, data governance, documentation, logging, transparency, human oversight, accuracy and robustness).

It is an educational prototype. It is not placed on the market or put into service and makes no real decisions. These documents follow the high-risk requirements as a learning exercise.
